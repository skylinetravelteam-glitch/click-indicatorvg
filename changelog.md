# v1.1.0

- Pick your own images in the mod settings.
- New settings: corner, size, horizontal/vertical margin, opacity, clicking-image scale, flip, only-show-while-clicking, player tracking, platformer buttons.
- Transparent borders are cropped automatically, so images sit flush in the corner.
- Works with macros / replay bots.
- Settings apply instantly, including from the pause menu.
- Re-pins itself if the window is resized or toggled to fullscreen.
